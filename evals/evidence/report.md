## M5: detection delay at a matched false-alarm rate

w drops 0.95 -> 0.85 (floor 0.9); 1000 null and 1000 change streams; false alarms over 5000 judged samples, target <= 5%. Delay in judged samples after the change.

| monitor | knob | false-alarm rate | median delay | p25-p75 | detected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | - | 0.000 | 5000 | - | 0.0% |
| fixed_threshold | window 450 | 0.042 | 254 | 194-322 | 99.9% |
| e1_matched | block 250, z 0.45, lambda 1.0 | 0.050 | 306 | 225-392 | 99.7% |
| e1_bonferroni | block 250, z 2.807, lambda 1.0 | 0.000 | 4113 | 1680-4576 | 47.9% |
| e1_ppi_pp_matched | block 250, z -0.95, lambda tuned (PPI++) | 0.045 | 852 | 325-4317 | 64.4% |
| e1_ppi_pp_bonferroni | block 250, z 2.807, lambda tuned (PPI++) | 0.000 | 4530 | 4264-4768 | 0.2% |

Faster at matched false alarms: **fixed_threshold**; M5 (E1 faster than the fixed threshold) **not met**.

Why `lambda = 1` for E1: PPI++ tunes lambda on human labels from *before* the change; with few losses at w = 0.95 it is about 0.3, so the estimate leans on the stale human mean (`e1_ppi_pp_*` rows). The pre-registered Bonferroni E1 (`e1_bonferroni`) is valid by construction but, with 20 looks at alpha/20, slow: that is the price E2 (anytime-valid sequences, future scope) would remove.

## Judge sensitivity: the gate with and without PPI

2000 decisions per row; floor 0.9; 200 human labels, 2000 judge-only pairs. A truly bad candidate (w = 0.86) declared **safe** is the dangerous error; a truly good one (w = 0.96) declared safe is right.

| judge | bad declared safe: PPI | judge only | good declared safe: PPI | judge only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| good | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.999 | 1.000 |
| mediocre | 0.001 | 0.000 | 0.986 | 0.000 |
| loss_blind | 0.001 | 0.943 | 0.991 | 1.000 |
| drifted | 0.177 | 0.939 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

`drifted`: the judge was calibrated (good) when the human labels were collected, then became loss-blind. The rectifier is stale, and PPI's protection shrinks: the reason cards go `stale` and §10.4 asks for fresh labels after any change.
